## Phase 3A — Versioned Evolution Assets and Runtime Materialization

Planned release target: `v0.2.0`.

One future vertical contract should close together:

1. `SkillDefinition` and immutable `SkillRevision` with source, commit/digest,
   provenance, dependencies, Runtime compatibility, capability/risk manifest,
   and `draft/candidate/active/archived/revoked` lifecycle.
2. Local creation and controlled local/git/inline import. Third-party content
   enters Candidate and cannot execute before validation and explicit activation.
3. Exact revision binding to existing AgentDefinition, TeamDefinition, or
   WorkPackage without creating a second registry or copying mutable assets into
   a Squad-private authority.
4. Codex and Pi Runtime-native materialization with source-lock verification,
   no overwrite of repository-owned Skills, cleanup, and adapter conformance.
5. Every Run freezes exact revision IDs and digests. In-flight Runs do not
   change when an asset is updated, archived, revoked, or rolled back.
6. Only permitted terminal Runs with accepted Evidence may propose Skill,
   Agent, Team, WorkPackage, or Strategy Candidates. Promotion remains
   Candidate-only and requires explicit activation.
7. Activation, archive, restore, revocation, and rollback append Journal facts;
   all catalogs and asset views remain rebuildable projections.
8. A controlled vertical canary proves import/create, validation, activation,
   binding, materialization, governed execution, Evidence, rollback, restart,
   and Projection rebuild without credential, raw Grant, or hidden-reasoning
   leakage.

### Preserved authority

- Event Journal remains the only state authority.
- TeamDefinition/TeamInstance remain the team authority; Squad may be a template
  or product-facing name only.
- WorkItem remains the governable execution unit; Issue/Mission may be an
  optional parent or facade only.
- Run/Attempt/generation remain the execution lineage.
- AgentGrant remains the capability authority; a RunToken may only be its
  short-lived transport credential.
- Evidence and independent verification remain required for acceptance.

## Phase 3B — Optional Governed Sandbox Isolation

Planned release target: `v0.2.1 experimental`.

This is a later, separately frozen boundary after Phase 3A acceptance. It may:

1. define a vendor-neutral `SandboxBackend` capability contract for `Create`,
   `Exec`, `Cancel`, `Pause`, `Resume`, `Destroy`, and
   `InspectCapabilities`;
2. run a bounded comparison spike for AgentENV, CubeSandbox, E2B Cloud, or
   another reviewed candidate across deployment, authentication, isolation,
   data residency, cost, cancellation, restart, and recovery;
3. select at most one backend for the first implementation;
4. expose remote Linux sandboxing as an explicit, default-off isolation tier;
5. fail closed when policy requires sandboxing and the backend, identity,
   generation, or secure channel is unavailable; and
6. complete one governed Runtime canary proving cancellation, crash/restart
   reconciliation, cleanup, no duplicate side effects, and no secret leakage.

The sandbox backend, scheduler, checkpoint, session, callback, or E2B-compatible
API never becomes a second state, execution, credential, or recovery authority.
Loom reconciles it from Journal and accepted artifact facts.

## Explicit exclusions

The queued v0.2.0/0.2.1 work does not authorize:

- full Multica alignment as a product goal;
- replacing Team with Squad, WorkItem with Issue/Task, or AgentGrant with a new
  token authority;
- fourteen full Runtime adapters or support claims based only on discovery or a
  stdin wrapper;
- an MCP Broker before at least two governed Runtime bindings prove a common
  unmet need;
- simultaneous AgentENV and CubeSandbox implementations;
- sandbox default-on, silent `--no-sandbox` downgrade, public sandbox API, or
  raw Provider credentials inside a sandbox;
- Marketplace, shared third-party assets, multi-user, Autopilot, standing
  orders, webhook triggers, or remote callback authority; or
- thin wrapper WorkItems for registry, writer, coordinator, adapter, scheduler,
  or one-call forwarding layers.

## Future freeze requirements

Before Phase 3A or Phase 3B implementation, a fresh contract must define:

- exact owned files and authority boundaries;
- migration/replay compatibility with zero unexplained data loss;
- deterministic, race, crash/restart, security, adapter-conformance, and live
  test matrices;
- observability derived from Journal correlation without a second authority;
- E2E performance budgets rather than vendor boot-time claims;
- rollback and cleanup rules; and
- an independent Contract Review PASS.
